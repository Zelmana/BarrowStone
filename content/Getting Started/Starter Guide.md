As a base introduction to the server, you should familiarize yourself with the terminology used. An understanding of basic [[RP Terminology]] is critical.

Barrowstone roleplay takes place within the Discord server. At any time you can contact a Guide via the @Guide role to ask for assistance.

# Starter Lore
After an understanding of roleplaying basics, the next step is to familiarize yourself with the starter lore. This allows you to create a character that is appropriately themed for the environment.

# Core Mechanics
An review of the core mechanics of Barrowstone is recommended before character creation and beginning roleplay as well. This will allow you to plan character ambitions and motives, as well as whether or not you would pursue joining a guild, creating your own faction, or remaining unaligned. Reviewing the [[Mechanics/index|Mechanics Category]] will prove helpful, especially to those who wish to create factions and manage assets.
