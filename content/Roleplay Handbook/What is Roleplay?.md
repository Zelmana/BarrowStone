### What is Roleplay?

Roleplay in its most generic sense is playing out a character within a story, often literally putting on a "role" as if in a play. Freeform roleplay extends this concept further, by asking the player to embody the character in unscheduled or unplanned sessions, without script or too many constraints. This, done with other characters, within a defined setting such as Barrowstone creates an evolving narrative that is both fun to engage in as well as read from an out of character perspective.

### Are there different levels of Roleplaying?

Roleplaying can take many forms, whether it is single player RPGs, tabletop gaming,, or community-based freeform RP described above taking place in MMOs. Barrowstone's roleplay format takes place within a Discord server.

Just as there are many different formats of roleplay, there are also varying qualities. To borrow terminology from *Space Station 13*, here are some example labels.

**Low Roleplay (LRP)**
Low Roleplay puts the least emphasis on quality roleplay. Characters are not limited in design or what they are allowed to do. A character may not even have a developed name, background, or motive. There is little care for remaining in character. Some in the community may have joke characters or names reflecting of the disregard for immersion.

**Medium Roleplay (MRP)**
Medium Roleplay puts an emphasis on remaining in character at all times. Characters are limited to fit in within the setting of the roleplay. It is encouraged that characters have a background and story. Canonical consistency between sessions may or may not exist, and often characters are one-off throwaways. Emotive or action descriptor text may take place, but are not regularly used. There may be in-character "metawords" which are stand ins for out-of-character things, or methods of describing out-of-character actions such as keybinds or processes. Generally, the line between OOC and IC are often blurry.

**Heavy Roleplay (HRP)**
Heavy Roleplay focuses on a high quality roleplay experience. Characters are not only limited but very grounded in their universe. Characters are developed not only to have a background but also may have ambitions and long-arcing storylines. Players create a delineation between in-character and out-of-character. Player writers create well thought out themes and tones, including emotive action descriptors.

BarrowStone aims to provide a place for HRP quality roleplay.
